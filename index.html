About Me

I build agentic AI systems that are efficient, reliable, and secure and private enough for real-world deployment.

I am a Staff Research Scientist on Visa Research's GenAI Trustworthy and Efficiency (GATE) team. My research spans system design, security and privacy, and large-scale GenAI systems.

Previously, I worked on cyber-physical systems and security, side-channel cryptanalysis, and wireless communications and networking. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Texas A&M University in 2019, and my M.S. and B.S. from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC).
